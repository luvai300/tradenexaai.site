Activate your 3% markup

  1. Open the Deriv application dashboard →.
  2. Select your registered app and make sure its type is OAuth. Add the exact redirect URL shown here.
  3. Enable the trade scope. The terminal requests trading access only.
  4. Set the app’s markup fee to 3% and save.
  5. Save your changes, connect Deriv, and test using a demo trading account first.

Markup is controlled by Deriv. Displaying 3% here does not enable commission. Set the fee in your Deriv app settings; commission collection has not yet been verified.
